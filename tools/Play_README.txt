DEAD SHIFT: WESTFIELD HIGH  (Godot build)

ALREADY SET UP? Replace DeadShift.pck with the new one, double-click Play_DeadShift.bat once.
After that you can just double-click DeadShift.exe (pin it to the taskbar if you like).

FIRST TIME
1. Download Godot 4.4.1 for Windows (the "Standard" 64-bit version) from
   https://godotengine.org/download/archive/4.4.1-stable/ , unzip it, and put
   Godot_v4.4.1-stable_win64.exe in this folder.
2. Double-click Play_DeadShift.bat. It makes DeadShift.exe (a copy of Godot that
   loads the game by itself) and starts it. From then on, DeadShift.exe is the game.

KEYBOARD + MOUSE
  WASD move, SHIFT sprint, SPACE jump, mouse look
  Left click fire, right click aim
  R reload (also restarts after you die)
  F buy / open doors / mystery box / feed Hammy / power / push the bookshelf /
    shock gate lever / hotwire the bus (30,000)
  E or G throw a grenade
  Q, 1, 2 or mouse wheel swap guns
  V knife (a kick if you're sprinting), L flashlight
  WILDCATS BAT (cafeteria stage, 1250): click to swing, keep clicking for the
    3-hit combo (sweep, backhand, overhead slam). V swings it too.
  F on a chair, bin, trophy or fire extinguisher picks it up; click to throw it
  ESC pause menu (settings live there), F11 fullscreen

CONTROLLER (Xbox layout)
  Left stick move (click it to sprint), right stick look
  RT fire, LT aim, A jump, X reload / use, B or right stick click knife,
  Y swap, RB grenade, D-pad up flashlight, Start pause
  Aim assist (Off/Low/Normal/Strong) and stick deadzone are in Settings.
  Button prompts switch to Xbox buttons as soon as you touch the controller.

Settings, high scores and unlocks are saved in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Dead Shift- Westfield High
